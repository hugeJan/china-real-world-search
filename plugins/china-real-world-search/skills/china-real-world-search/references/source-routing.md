# Source Routing for Mainland China Public Services

Read this file only after the main Skill's scope gate passes.

The goal is not to search every China-local source. The goal is to choose the system that naturally generates the decisive fact for an in-scope government or state public-service task.

## 1. Four evidence lanes

### Rule lane

Use for eligibility, materials, statutory fees, official terminology, and responsible authority:

- competent ministry, commission, bureau, regulator, or local authority;
- current government service guide;
- formal notice, gazette, or policy document;
- current operator tariff/service rule where the operator defines the public service.

### Execution lane

Use for how the user can act now:

- current government or operator portal;
- official app, public account, mini program, service hall, branch, station, counter, self-service terminal, or hotline documentation;
- current migration, suspension, restoration, relocation, or operational notice;
- concrete third-party channel only when it serves an already in-scope public-service process.

### Reality lane

Use when practical current conditions can change the recommendation:

- map/POI data for an outlet, station, entrance, route, or service area;
- live transaction or timetable state when the host can actually inspect it;
- recent independent user reports for queues, transfer friction, failed entries, or current availability;
- the user's own firsthand observation.

### History lane

Use for old or removed services:

- government gazettes and original PDFs;
- archived official pages;
- migration/replacement notices;
- contemporary references to the old entry.

Historical evidence must be time-scoped.

## 2. Government and administrative services

For rules and legal requirements, prefer the competent authority and the current local service guide.

For execution, separately check:

- provincial/city/district platform;
- responsible office or service-hall notice;
- current appointment or online application entry;
- official hotline documentation;
- actual service point and jurisdiction coverage.

Do not assume the national rule describes every local execution detail.

## 3. Public institutions

For a public hospital, public school, examination body, or other public institution:

- verify the exact institution/campus/office;
- use the institution and competent system for official rules;
- use current official registration/admission/service channels for execution;
- use maps only for location, entrance, campus, and route facts;
- use community reports for practical experience, not as authority for eligibility or fees.

Do not activate this Skill for a private institution merely because it is locally important.

## 4. State-owned public-service operators

A state-owned entity is in scope only for its public-facing service operation.

### Utilities

Examples of decisive sources:

- current operator service guide and tariff;
- outage/maintenance notice;
- account opening, transfer, meter, payment, or service-area rule;
- local branch/service hall.

### Telecommunications

Use current operator materials for:

- SIM replacement, cancellation, portability, broadband installation/move, account or identity requirements;
- tariff, service area, channel, outlet, and current operational changes.

Do not use this Skill for branded phones, firmware, flashing, Root, device repair, or ordinary app troubleshooting.

### Postal service

Use current postal/operator sources for:

- service type, outlet, hours, addressing rules, prohibited items, fees, tracking procedure, or complaint route.

### State-bank routine service

Use current bank/branch sources for:

- account/card replacement, social-security-card service, branch capability, hours, required materials, fees, and routine counter/online procedures.

Do not use this Skill for stock analysis, wealth-product selection, fund/insurance recommendations, or general investment advice.

## 5. Public transport

Choose the source by the fact:

- railway schedule, fare, purchase rule, and inventory → authoritative railway transaction/operator surface when accessible;
- flight and airport operations → operating airline, airport, civil-aviation authority, or authoritative transaction surface when accessible;
- metro/bus/ferry/public coach operations → operator, transport authority, station/port, or recognized ticketing system;
- disruption and first/last service → current operator or authority notice;
- actual transfer geometry and walking burden → current map/POI data;
- station-entry or transfer friction → operator guidance plus recent reality evidence.

If direct inventory or app state is unavailable, do not present it as live-confirmed.

Ordinary driving/walking directions without a public-transport dependency are out of scope. Taxi or ride-hailing may be a supporting transfer leg but does not trigger the Skill by itself.

## 6. Third-party channels

A third party can be relevant without being the trigger.

Use broad web, app-store, platform-native search, provider pages, maps, or community reports to discover a named candidate. Then verify:

- operator identity;
- current usability;
- target-process compatibility;
- official relationship;
- locality and date.

Provider marketing does not define the official rule or prove official designation.

## 7. Platform capability rule

A source category is not a tool capability.

- Claim direct WeChat, Alipay, 12306, map-app, hospital-system, or operator-app inspection only when the host actually exposed that surface.
- Otherwise use accessible official/web/secondary evidence.
- State the material live-state gap plainly.
- Never invent menus, slots, inventory, prices, or issuer details.

## 8. Sources that do not change scope

Private maps, search engines, social platforms, local-life sites, and third-party service pages may support an in-scope task.

Their availability does not expand the Plugin into:

- consumer technology;
- shopping or product research;
- private local-life recommendations;
- company/project investigation;
- finance or investment;
- general China-local search.

The scope is determined before source routing.
